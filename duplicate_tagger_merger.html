Duplicate tagger & merger

Upload a CRM contact export (CSV or XLSX). Rows get scanned for name and contact-info matches, tagged, and — where the match is certain — merged into the newer record.
CRM: Suspected Duplicate

Name match (incl. nicknames) or identical email address. Flagged for review, not merged.

CRM: Merged

Name match plus an identical email, phone, or address. Older row's data folded into the newer row.

CRM: Void Dupe

The older row in a confirmed merge. Removed from the main file, kept in a separate void list.

Assumptions this tool makes
  • Columns are auto-detected by name (case-insensitive): anything containing "first"+"name", "last"+"name", "email", "phone", "address", "tag", "group", "note"/"background", "created".
  • Name matching uses a built-in common-nickname list (Rebecca/Becky, Richard/Dick, James/Jim, etc.) plus exact matches on last name.
  • Merges require an exact match on email, phone (digits only, last 10 compared), or address (punctuation/case-insensitive) — never on name alone.
  • The newest row is chosen by parsing your "Created At" column as a date. Rows with an unparseable or missing date are treated as the oldest.
  • Tags/Groups/Notes/Background fields are combined (comma-separated, duplicates skipped) into the surviving row.
  • Other contact fields (phone/email/address) fill blanks on the surviving row; every distinct value across the whole merge cluster is preserved in "(2)", "(3)", etc. columns rather than being dropped.
  • This runs entirely in your browser — no data leaves your machine.
